# Office sprites: prompts

One prompt per character. Image AIs are bad at exact pixel grids, so treat the result as a draft: if a pixel-art tool (Aseprite, PixelLab, Piskel) is available, clean it up there to a **128 × 576** PNG. The game can run on the placeholder sprites until then.

### 1. Save as `assets/sprites/laras.png`

**Attach:** `assets/characters/laras/_reference.png`

```text
Pixel art sprite sheet for a cozy top-down life-sim game (Stardew Valley style). A grid of 4 columns and 12 rows; every cell is one 32x48 pixel frame of the same character, feet at the bottom of the cell, centered. 1-pixel dark outline, limited palette, no anti-aliasing, transparent background. Rows from top to bottom: 1 walking toward the camera (4 frames), 2 walking left (4), 3 walking right (4), 4 walking away (4), 5 standing idle (2 frames, the second one blinking), 6 sitting and typing seen from behind (2), 7 drinking from a cup (2), 8 eating a snack (2), 9 looking at a tablet (2), 10 yawning (2), 11 a small bow (2), 12 cheering with arms up (2). Empty cells stay transparent.

Character: Laras, a calm, warm, confident team lead. Long straight dark-brown hair falling past the shoulders with two front locks over the shoulders, side-swept bangs, warm brown eyes. Navy tailored blazer with darker lapels, white blouse, two small gold buttons. Small, cute but adult proportions (about 2.5 heads tall); keep her hair shape and outfit colors recognisable at this size.
```
